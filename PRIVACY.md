# SWFTC Charts Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 5, 2026**

SWFTC Charts ("the App") is a Reddit application designed to provide cryptocurrency market information and automatically generate market chart posts for participating Reddit communities.

## Information the App Uses

SWFTC Charts may access limited Reddit information necessary to operate within a subreddit where the App has been installed. This may include the subreddit name and information necessary to create, manage, flair, or pin posts produced by the App.

The App does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, payment information, private messages, or other personally identifying information from Reddit users.

## App Configuration and Storage

The App may store operational configuration and state using Reddit's Devvit storage services. This may include settings such as the selected cryptocurrency, chart period, posting time, whether automatic posting is enabled, and identifiers necessary to manage posts created by the App.

This information is used solely to provide and maintain the App's functionality.

## External Services

The App uses CoinGecko to obtain publicly available cryptocurrency market information, including prices, market capitalization, trading volume, historical prices, and related market statistics.

The App uses QuickChart to generate chart images from cryptocurrency market data. Market information and chart configuration data may be transmitted to QuickChart to render these images.

The App does not intentionally transmit Reddit usernames, user-generated Reddit content, private messages, or other personal information about Reddit users to CoinGecko or QuickChart.

Use of these services may also be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## Data Sharing and Sale

SWFTC Charts does not sell personal information.

The App does not intentionally share Reddit user information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

## Data Retention

Operational configuration and state may remain stored through Reddit's Devvit infrastructure while the App is installed.

Public posts created by the App remain subject to Reddit's normal content-management and deletion mechanisms.

## Security

Reasonable efforts are made to limit the App's access and processing to information necessary for its stated functionality.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.

## Contact

Questions concerning this Privacy Policy or SWFTC Charts may be directed to the developer through Reddit.
